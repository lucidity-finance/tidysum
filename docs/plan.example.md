# My money plan (example)

> An example of the plan Tidysum reads, for a made-up person. It shows the shape, not what you
> should do: it is not financial advice (see [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md)). Copy it to
> `plan.md` in the repo root (git ignores that file), rewrite it in your own words, and load it
> with `pnpm tidysum plan set plan.md`.

Last reviewed: 2026-01-15. Change it once a year, in January, never during a market drop.

## Mission

Money works in the background so I can focus on my job and my life. Investing is not a second
job: it runs on autopilot, and a market drop changes nothing. Tidysum watches the details and
tells me only what needs a decision.

## Goals

1. **Financially independent by 60.** Invested savings reach 25 times my yearly spending.
2. **Save at least 15% of gross pay for retirement**, counting the employer match.
3. **Emergency reserve of about 3 months of spending** in a high-yield savings account. Pay every
   card in full and carry no debt.
4. **Reach and hold the target mix** using new money only, without selling.

## Target mix

Counted across every account, including the 401(k).

| Bucket               | Target | Where it is held                                   |
| -------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| US stocks            | 60%    | 401(k) US index funds; a total US market fund      |
| International stocks | 30%    | 401(k) international index fund; a total intl fund |
| Bonds and cash       | 10%    | 401(k) bond index fund and cash                    |

## Where each dollar goes

In this order. A step starts only once the one above it is full.

1. **Bills and cards** are paid from checking, in full. Checking keeps $3,000 on top.
2. **401(k) up to the full employer match.**
3. **Emergency reserve** until it holds 3 months of spending.
4. **Roth IRA** up to the yearly limit.
5. **401(k) up to the yearly limit.**
6. **Everything else above the buffer** goes to a taxable brokerage account, into the stock index
   split Tidysum names.

## Rules

1. New money does the rebalancing. I don't sell to rebalance.
2. New money buys broad, low-cost index funds only.
3. Everything is automatic: payroll for the 401(k), a payday transfer for the rest.
4. I don't watch markets. I look at Tidysum at most once a quarter.
5. No margin, options, leverage or loans.
6. A swap for cheaper funds copies exactly what the fund holds, from its fact sheet, sleeve by
   sleeve. If the holdings aren't known, there is no swap.
7. Nothing moves without my approval, one action at a time.

## Accounts and their jobs

| Account            | Job                                       |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Checking           | Paycheck lands here; bills; the buffer    |
| High-yield savings | Emergency reserve                         |
| Two credit cards   | Everyday spending, paid in full           |
| 401(k)             | Pre-tax retirement savings, steps 2 and 5 |
| Roth IRA           | Step 4                                    |
| Taxable brokerage  | Step 6; broad stock index funds only      |
