The wealth manager's playbook, without the wealth manager's fee.

Tidysum connects all your accounts, finds where your money is costing you and how much a year, and has your own AI agent carry out the fixes you approve. You only step in to sign in or press Submit.

The demo household. Every name and number is made up.

Every finding has a price

Not "your allocation drifted", but what it costs you a year and the exact fix. Tidysum looks at cash, cards, fees, debt, your 401(k), IRAs and new money to invest.

"Send your paycheck to a high-yield account instead of checking that pays 0%: about $1,100 a year more in interest."
"With what you spend on groceries and dining, this card earns about $310 a year more than the one you use."
"Your 401(k) fund charges 0.65%. The plan's own index funds copy it for 0.04%."
"$18,000 is sitting above your cash cushion. Put it into your index-fund mix, here's the split."

Illustrative examples. Real findings use your accounts, your rules and published rates.

You decide. Your agent does the legwork.

Tidysum itself is plain, exact code with no AI inside. The thinking and the clicking come from the agent you already use, and nothing happens without your yes.

What 1% a year really costs

Most advisers charge a percentage of everything you have, every year: a median of about 1% on the first $1 million (Kitces Research, via SmartAsset). Compounded over 30 years, that's about a quarter of what you'd otherwise end up with.

Built to be safe with your money

  • Nothing moves without you. Only you approve, on Tidysum's own page. On a trade, your agent stops at the review screen.
  • Agents can't see secrets. They read through a read-only database role with row-level security, never your bank tokens.
  • Your data stays home. It lives in a Postgres database on your own computer, not in anyone's cloud.
  • No referral money. Products are ranked only by what they'd gain you, from published terms. No affiliate links.
  • Everything is recorded. Every proposal, approval and agent step goes in an append-only audit log.
  • Exact money. Amounts are decimal strings in code and numeric in the database, never floating point.

Get started

Try it on the made-up household first. You need Node 22.19 or newer, pnpm and Docker. 

git clone https://github.com/lucidity-finance/tidysum.git && cd tidysum
pnpm install
pnpm demo        # then open http://127.0.0.1:8090

Then connect your own banks, cards and brokerages with a free Plaid account (its free plan covers 10 institutions), or ask your agent to set it all up for you. Full quick start.

Tidysum is software you run yourself. It is not a financial adviser, and nothing it shows is financial, tax or legal advice. You approve every action, and your own agent carries it out. Disclaimer.
