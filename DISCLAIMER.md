## You decide, and your agent acts

Tidysum moves no money by itself. It finds things, puts a price on them and drafts an action.
**You** approve each action, and **your own AI agent** (one you choose, configure and run) carries
it out with its own tools, on your accounts, under your sign-ins. That means:

- Every action is your decision and your responsibility, including what your agent does or fails
  to do while carrying it out.
- AI agents make mistakes. Watch what yours does, check each result with the institution, and stop

- Banks, brokerages, card issuers and plan providers have their own terms for automated access
  and third-party agents. Following them is up to you.
- Your data stays where you run Tidysum. Keeping that computer, your database, your `.env` and
  your API keys secure is up to you.
